## GTA 6 LITE prototype

A small, original 2D canvas city sandbox. Complete the age gate, choose one of eight regions, then explore from the local safehouse. Use **WASD** to move, **Shift** to sprint, **E** to interact, **C** to change camera, **P** to open the phone, and **V** for character dialogue. The activity board and nearby city landmarks open the prototype's services and activities.

### Prototype notes

- This is a fictional, single-page 2D prototype—not GTA VI, an official Rockstar product, or a full 3D open-world game.
- Age verification is a client-side date check for the demo only; it does not verify identity or enforce access securely.
- Region, language labels, and safehouse text are local presentation choices. Weather is randomized locally, and the displayed clock uses the device's system time; there is no live weather or location service.
- Game progress and most activities are temporary and are not saved. Some interactions are lightweight simulations rather than complete game systems.
- Google Fonts are loaded externally; the game itself has no image or model asset dependency.
